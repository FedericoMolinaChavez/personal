# Consulting website extension

Mode: Persuade. Scope: homepage and the AI systems audit and fractional CTO routes approved in website-change-plan.md.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Help a visitor choose a relevant engagement and take one clear next step. Preserve the personal consulting identity rather than introduce a new brand.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the warm ivory ground, clay primary, sage secondary, Bricolage Grotesque typography, generous whitespace and soft corners from the existing implementation.

STORY: Understand the business outcome, see existing project context, understand the scope, then book a conversation. Fixed-price checkout remains distinct from scoping.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-aligned large outcome headline, readable introduction and a clear booking action. On service pages, an adjacent inset summary explains the engagement; narrower screens stack these regions. Home proof links follow the hero.

FORM: A precisely specified extension of the current site and the approved page plan; no concept tournament or replacement visual world. No new image assets or unverified metrics.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Open facts: additional fixed prices, audit duration, client metrics, session lengths and any future scorecard scoring calibration. Publish no invented answers.
